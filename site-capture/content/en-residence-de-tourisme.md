## INZOVU HÔTEL BY ODALYS
Inzovu Hotel delivers much more than simple accommodation: it is a comprehensive, integrated solution designed to meet the needs of companies and business travellers.
Covering more than 5,000 sqm and 95 guest rooms, our 5-star business tourist residence blends elegance with comfort and functionality. Each spacious fl at comes with climate control, and is set up to provide a unique experience, whether guests are here for a short, medium or long stay.
In line with Odalys standards, we have envisioned our communal spaces as genuine social hubs – a swimming pool with panoramic views of Kigali, relaxation areas and high-end amenities – to be real community spaces that enrich the guest experience.
