## Discover Inzovu Mall
Anglais
Designed as a true living space with a modern and innovative design, this new area will offer visitors a unique experience in the country’s largest shopping mall.
Located in the heart of the Kimihurura district, a business and commercial area close to the city center, Inzovu Mall will offer breathtaking views of the lush hills and valleys of Kigali, as well as the iconic dome of the Kigali Conference Centre (CCK). A sustainable building, Inzovu Mall will utilize energy-efficient materials and technologies, reducing its greenhouse gas emissions and contributing to Rwanda’s efforts to mitigate climate change. The climate-conscious center has been awarded EDGE certification for its adherence to green building standards.
This large-scale project, spanning over 30,000 m², includes more than 9,000 m² of retail space, over 4,000 m² of leisure and dining options, a 95-room Odalys business tourism residence, and office space. Inzovu Mall embodies the new generation of shopping centers thanks to an exclusive offering, combining shops, restaurants, leisure and services in an environmentally friendly setting.
## The Duval Group in Africa
As a responsible investor, the Duval Group is committed to Africa, to meet the continent’s challenges. The Group is not only present with the Inzovu project in Rwanda.
The Group leverages its expertise to meet the needs of African governments and the private sector by developing high value-added projects. Backed by 30 years of know-how and experience in France, the Group is present in Africa as a developer, investor and operator. It is developing commercial real estate and affordable housing programs in Côte d’Ivoire, Rwanda, Togo, Cameroon and Senegal. Some mixed-use projects are developed in partnership with Odalys, a Groupe Duval subsidiary, which is pursuing its diversification policy, particularly in French-speaking Africa with city-center hotel apartments for business tourism.
