## INZOVU
CONFERENCE VENUE:
EXTRAORDINARY
EVENT AMENITIES
Inzovu Conference Venue was created to be a hybrid facility with a mix of offices, coworking spaces and a conference centre.
Sustainability and modern design are central to this concept. The site uses eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient systems, reflecting our vision for responsible event management in East Africa.
The business centre, along with our coworking areas, give professionals an allencompassing work environment where comfort meets flexibility and execution. With close to 6,000 sqm of space, it features modular office space, high-end technical equipment, dedicated event planning assistance and a whole floor devoted solely to conferences, including two large, fully equipped rooms.
In this way, Inzovu Conference Venue provides an optimal setting for companies and business travellers, where they can hold events, work efficiently and operate in an environment that  is simultaneously functional, elegant and conducive to success.
