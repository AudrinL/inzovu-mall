01The idea

Shops , dining , leisure and a five-star hotel — one living space on a Kigali hilltop, a few steps from the Convention Centre.

Inzovu means elephant in Kinyarwanda. The building carries the name honestly: a white ribbon of terraces curling around a plaza, planted with the hills' own green, opening onto the dome of the Kigali Convention Centre.

Developed by Groupe Duval, operated with Odalys, EDGE-certified for its energy and water design. Works started in Q3 2023. The doors open in the first quarter of 2027.
